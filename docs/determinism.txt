General:

Assume GPU is nondeterministic.
Avoid functions with nondeterministic results like pairs in relevant places, unless used in such a way that it makes no difference.
When multithreading, ensure that any combining calculations like sums over the results are done in deterministic order with deterministic results.

Use dmath module/library for maths functions that can vary across platforms.
Assume all platforms are IEEE 754-compliant (this makes +/*- and sqrt safe).
